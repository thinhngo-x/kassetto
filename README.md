# Kassetto

A self-hosted music player with a recommender that learns *you*: session-aware, explains every pick, and
gets better from what you finish, skip or pass over.

**Status:** pre-release. Work in progress towards v0.1; not ready for use yet.

> Kassetto plays music from YouTube Music's public catalogue through your own server. Downloading from YouTube
> is against YouTube's terms of service. Kassetto is meant for personal, self-hosted use only; you are responsible
> for how you use it. It is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.
